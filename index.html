About

Light, shadow,
and the frame between.

Geoffrey Kenner is a Director and Director of Photography specialising in Feature Film narrative. His work is rooted in the language of photochemical cinema — the warmth of tungsten spilling through practical lights, the grain of a fast emulsion, the quiet bloom of a lens catching the edge of a flame.

Drawing from the visual traditions of Kodachrome colour science and the lush, saturated greens of Fujifilm's legacy stocks, Geoffrey crafts images that feel at once timeless and deeply alive. Every frame is considered as a painting in motion — foreground rendered sharp and tactile against backgrounds that breathe and shift in soft focus.

He works across the full spectrum of the filmmaking craft: from the conception of a visual language in pre-production, through the intimate collaboration of principal photography, to the final colour grade where each scene finds its ultimate emotional register.
